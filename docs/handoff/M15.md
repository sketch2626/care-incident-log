# Handoff: M15, cards and messages to family: research, plan and boards (second account, branch `other/M15`)

Read `docs/handoff/_common.md` first (it sends you to `docs/mission.md`). Then read the M15 item in `docs/mary-roadmap.md`, which has Andy's words and decisions. Also read `docs/skills-design.md` (each feature is a skill), the top of `memory_core.py` (dated memories), `skills/pictures/` (how she shows things), and the approved round 7 boards in `mockups/mary/round7/` for the look.

**Why (Andy, 2026-10-31):** "I see that it's your son's birthday coming up next week. Would you like for me to help you make him a card that we can send to him?" And messaging in general: "will you please help me send a message to my grandson?" This ties residents to their people outside. Older folks with a social profile are usually on Facebook Messenger, so that matters as much as email.

**This job is research, a plan and boards. No app code.**

1. **Research** (web; give sources and dates, and keep what's true today separate from guesses):
   - **Email from a resident's Windows laptop,** attaching a card picture: what sign-in works now for Gmail, Outlook.com and Yahoo (app passwords, OAuth, a device-code flow), the simplest one-time setup a helper can do, and keeping the secret safe (for example Windows Credential Manager, never plain text).
   - **Facebook Messenger from a personal account:** is there any official way for an app to send to a friend? What's allowed, what breaks Meta's rules, and the risk of an account being locked. Find the best honest flow: open the chat with that person with the message ready (prefilled if any supported way exists, otherwise ready to paste), with the card picture, and the resident taps Send.
   - **Others residents use:** WhatsApp (click-to-chat links), texting through Windows Phone Link with their own phone, and anything else common with people over 65.
   - **Image generation:** Gemini's image models, paid-tier price per image, privacy terms on the paid tier (Andy: residents will be on a paid tier for privacy), and safety filters. Also a free, offline set of card designs drawn in the app.
2. **The plan:** `docs/messaging-plan.md`, in plain words. Cover:
   - **The flows:** the birthday reminder (once, about a week ahead, from a dated memory), making a card, a plain message with no card, and sending each way.
   - **Who does what:** what Mary does by herself, and what the resident taps or confirms. Nothing is ever sent without the resident's clear yes.
   - **Contacts:** where a family member's email or Facebook name is kept, only if the resident wants it, and "forget" really removes it.
   - **The rest:** privacy (what goes to Google or Meta), costs, a build list (items, "done when", models), and "Decisions for Andy" with your recommendation on each.
3. **Boards:** `mockups/mary/round8/`, in the same format as round 7 (`.dc.html` files, `canvas.json`, the round 6/7 stylesheets, 1280x800 with Mary beside the content). Draw:
   - (a) her birthday reminder and the start of the card;
   - (b) choosing a design;
   - (c) the card with the resident's words;
   - (d) choosing who and how to send it (email or Messenger);
   - (e) the Messenger handoff: exactly what the resident sees and taps;
   - (f) email sent;
   - (g) a plain message to a grandson with no card.

   Residents are 65-95: text at least 20px, targets at least 64px, grown-up wording, no cutesy talk (`docs/mission.md`). Use one shared fictional family. No real names.

**Stay out of** all code files; only docs and `mockups/` change. **Models:** Opus does the research and the plan, and an Opus coder draws the boards. Haiku only for wording passes. Checkpoint after each part.

**Finish:** commit `WAITING: M15 plan and boards ready for Andy` with a short report: the honest answer on Messenger, the email setup in one line, image costs, the decisions for Andy, and the board list. Then stop.
