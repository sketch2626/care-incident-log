# Character concepts (M24, 2026-11-04)

Concept pictures Andy made with Nano Banana from the hub's prompts, for picking the look before any 3D is drawn. They show the target, not the final art.

- `01-radio-head-too-scary.webp`, `02-wood-mannequin-too-scary.webp`: what not to do. A grille over the mouth reads as a gag. A carved-wood human shape reads as a mannequin or puppet. A photo-real finish makes both uncanny.
- `03-lineup-six.webp`: six companions. **Andy kept the radio** (far right) as a prop on the porch table, and **the lantern** as a hanging porch light.
- `04-lineup-pairs-sam-picked.webp`: three pairs. **Sam is the second character**, the tall robot in faded tractor-red enamel with an olive feed-store cap. The hound (far right) is Sam's dog, and the gray cat is Mary's.
