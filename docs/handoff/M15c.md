**Why:** the cards and plain messages exist now (M15b), and the email core is ready (M15d core). This piece lets a resident actually send one. Andy put the smooth path this way: the card is on screen, they say "now send it", and Mary just sends it, with five seconds to change their mind.

**Build:**
1. **Contacts, in the `messages` skill's own store.**
   - What's kept: name, relationship, email, and a Facebook username or phone number. It stays on the laptop, apart from the care log, and goes in no report.
   - Gemini gets the names only, never the addresses. The core looks up the address when sending.
   - After the first send she asks once: "Keep his address for next time?" No is fine, and she never asks about that person again.
   - With no saved address, the resident or a helper can say or type one. Read it back letter by letter for a spoken one, since speech gets addresses wrong.
2. **The hard-wired yes.** The send never rests on Gemini's word.
   - The core sends only if the card or message and the person were on screen, Mary had said where it goes and that they'll have five seconds (board D2), and then a plain "send it" or the D2b tap came back.
   - "Okay" or "sounds good" in the middle of a chat doesn't count. Neither does a hint from Gemini, or a tool call alone.
   - After two failed tries to make them out, one quiet "Send it" button appears (board D2b). That's the only fallback.
3. **The Undo (boards D3 and G2).**
   - "Going to Mark's email.", then a big Undo counting 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. She stays quiet during the count. "Stop" or "undo" by voice works too.
   - Nothing leaves the laptop until the count ends. Only then does the core mint the one-time ticket and call `ctx.send_mail` as its last step. A retry gets a new ticket.
   - Then "Sent to Mark." and the time (board F).
4. **What happens after (board F).** She offers only the routes that are set up. For now that's printing; Messenger is M15e. If email isn't set up for that person, she goes straight to the routes that are.
5. **Safety.** She won't help send money, passwords or Social Security numbers. If a message asks for them, she pauses and says so plainly. She never writes to anyone on her own, never sends on a schedule, and never reads replies.
6. **Forget really forgets.** "Forget Mark's address" removes it. "Forget Mark" removes the contact, his dates and his address from `mail.log`. Memory's `forget` and `forget_name` reach the contact store, and a test proves it.
7. **The notes from M15d core:**
   - One send has 12 s, so keep card pictures small, about 600 KB or less in all, or give the send its own longer time outside the skill's limit.
   - A connection cut after the server took the message can report a failure. Say "I'm not sure it went" rather than retry on your own.

**Tests** use the fake SMTP server and fake Gemini. They prove:
- nothing sends without the yes, and a chatty "okay" doesn't count;
- Undo at any second stops it;
- a ticket is used once;
- "keep his address" is asked once;
- forget reaches contacts and `mail.log`;
- money and password requests pause.
