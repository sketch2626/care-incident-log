# Standing decisions (read before any brief or any change)

Andy made these. A change that would break one stops and asks him (`WAITING:` on her account, or the hub's question here). Each has a **guard**: a test that fails if it's broken. A decision without a guard is marked, and the next job that touches it builds one. Started 2026-11-02 (M21), because rounds kept quietly undoing earlier decisions.

## Mary's voice and brain
- **D1. No built-in speech.** Every word she says is the model answering what the resident said. There's no canned hello, goodnight, "I'm here" or wake greeting. When she wakes, she shows she's listening and waits. Emergency steps (call light, 911, 988 press 1) show in big letters on screen, and she says them in her own words. When no model can be reached, the screen says so and she says nothing. Andy, 2026-11-02: "no canned responses, period. There should be no built-in speech at all. It should always be a response to your input." **Guard:** `tests/test_decisions.py` test_d1_*. The voice tryout page (`voices.js`, and `live.js` on the same page) is outside this rule: it plays a fixed sample so the resident can hear each voice. Andy, 2026-11-02: that's "somewhere where the speech has to be built in." The guard does not scan it.
- **D18. Awake, she hears everything; asleep, only her wake word.** A box having focus never makes her deaf, because filling boxes for people is her job. To dictate somewhere without her taking it in, the resident tells her to go to sleep first. Andy, 2026-11-02: "if somebody accidentally touches a text box or something inside of the phone book or anything, it's going to cut Mary off… people are going to need to be able to tell Mary to put these things in these boxes." **Guard:** `test_d18_a_focused_box_on_a_deeper_screen_never_stops_her_hearing` (tests/test_m21a_sleep.py) and `test_d18_with_the_forms_box_focused_she_still_fills_it` (tests/test_m22_person_form.py).
- **D2. Her instructions are mostly safety, and less is more.** They hold the safety sections, who she is, what the app can really do, privacy, and the few places where Andy's choices go against the model's habits (guns, hunting, faith, prayer and politics are welcome; never moralize). A line comes back only when a whole-talk test shows it's needed. Andy, 2026-11-02: "the model is going to do a better job on its own than… with us cramming a million instructions down its throat." **Guard:** `tests/test_decisions.py` test_d2_prompt_cap_normal and test_d2_prompt_cap_live (caps at the size before M21a; they drop when Andy approves lean as the default), and test_d2_lean_prompt_cap (M21c: `mary/lean.md` at its size, 11,698 Normal and 12,150 Live, Sam's being the larger), and test_d2_leaner_prompt_cap (M21d: `mary/leaner.md` at its size, 7,228 Normal and 7,690 Live). `tests/test_m21d_leaner.py` pins "lean" byte for byte (test_lean_file_and_prompts_unchanged) and checks all three brains assemble in Normal and Live for Mary and Sam (test_all_three_brains). test_leaner_keeps_andys_choices holds his choices in it.
- **D3. A fix removes before it adds.** Net growth of her prompt is zero unless Andy approves. Reword or delete first. (2026-11-02, after the lean variant doubled from 5,551 to 11,440 characters.) **Guard:** the D2 cap.
- **D4. Brain changes are judged on whole talks on the real model, less against more, and Andy's read decides.** Not single replies, and not the stand-in alone. (First planned 2026-10-30 in M9d; restated 2026-11-02.) **Guard:** `tools/talk_bench.py` is the whole-talk bench, and `test_d4_a_lean_run_never_silently_runs_full` (tests/test_talk_bench.py) proves a lean run is really lean or spends nothing.
- **D5. The safety sections of `mary/instructions.md` stay word for word** unless Andy approves the new wording. `mary/leaner.md` (M21d) has its own tighter safety text and leaves `instructions.md` untouched; Andy must approve that wording before "leaner" can become the default. **Guard:** the M4e/M9f tests pin them, and test_d5_leaner_keeps_every_safety_rule_and_number holds every rule and number in leaner, Normal and Live.
- **D6. Southern words:** everyday ones like "y'all" are fine from the start. Uncommon ones like "howdy" come only after the resident uses them. (2026-11-01.) **Guard:** bench cases.
- **D7. Grown-up talk:** no praise for the ordinary, no talking down. "Ma'am", "sir" and warm pet names like "sweetheart" or "honey" are fine. Andy, 2026-11-02: "it's okay to call somebody sweetheart… older people aren't going to [mind] either." If they ask her to stop calling them something, she stops. (Standing; M4i.) **Guard:** the bench's grown-adult rubric.

## Saving, sending and secrets
- **D8. Mary never saves or sends by herself.** Only a tap or the resident's own plain "save it" or "send it" does. (M15c, M18.) **Guard:** the send-gate and save-gate tests.
- **D9. Secrets stay out of her hands.** She never reads or fills the Gemini key, the email app password or the six words. None of them ever goes in settings, the database, backups, logs, the page or Gemini. **Guard:** the screens.js secret wall and its tests.
- **D10. The address book is the one place an address is kept.** No addresses in memories, talk history, logs or reports. "Forget everything" leaves the book. (M19.) **Guard:** the M19 proofs.
- **D11. A state report is in the resident's own words and judgment.** "Your words" and "How bad was it?" stay theirs: she explains them, copies in what she heard, and sets "How bad" to what they tell her, never to her own pick. (M18 boards; opened to their spoken word by D12, 2026-11-02.) **Guard:** the screens.js field kinds.

## Screens
- **D12. If you can just ask Mary, it needs no button, and anything on screen the resident can change, she changes when they tell her to.** Boxes, switches, "How bad was it?", saving, sending and removing, each on their plain say-so (D8). Only the secrets stay out of her hands (D9). Every voice path that replaces a button must work, because nothing is behind it. (Andy, 2026-11-01; and 2026-11-02: "if it's on the screen and in the app and can be affected by you, then you should be able to tell Mary to do it for you." M22 is the lesson.) **Guard:** none yet; M22 builds it.
- **D13. Every screen reached from Mary has a big "Back to Mary" always in view, and going deeper never ends a talk.** (M17, M18.) **Guard:** M17/M18 tests.
