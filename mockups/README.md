# Mockups

Design boards. Static, not app code. `.dc.html` files are board files.

## First screens (history)
`a-tiles.html`, `b-big-buttons.html`, `c-tiles-with-summary.html` and `index.html` are the first screen designs.
Design C was approved 2026-10-23 and is what the home screen follows.

## Mary's look and screens: `mary/`
`face-boards.html` shows Mary's face. The rounds below are in order.
| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `round2/` | Faces and moods, and the call, message and emergency screens. |
| `round4/` | Choosing her look, first hello, his and hers faces. |
| `round5/` | Games: word search, solitaire, tic-tac-toe. |
| `round6/` | Captions and the one button (M3). Approved 2026-10-30. |
| `round7/` | Word search (M7a). |
| `round8/` | Cards and messages to family (M15b redraw). |
| `round9/` | Mary comes along into deeper screens (M18). |
| `round10/` | A secure address book (M19). |
| `concepts/` | The M24 cast Andy picked: Mary and Sam as 1950s robots on a porch swing. |

`round8-src/`, `round9-src/` and `round10-src/` hold `build.py` and the art that build rounds 8 to 10.

## Current and history
Rounds 8, 9 and 10 are the newest, and they are the designs the cards (M15b), deeper screens (M18) and address book (M19) were built from.
The M24 porch rounds (11 and 12) live on their branches until they're merged. Rounds 2 to 10 still document the screens that are built, but their face look is being replaced by the porch scene (M24). Rounds 2 to 7 and the top-level boards are history. Some of those ideas, such as solitaire and the call screen, aren't built.
