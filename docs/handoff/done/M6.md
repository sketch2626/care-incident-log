# Handoff: M6 wake word, "Hey Mary" / "Hey Sam" (second account, branch `other/M6`)

Read `docs/handoff/_common.md` first. Then read only these: `docs/mary-roadmap.md` M5 and M6; how `speech.py` opens the mic (sounddevice) and its listen routes in `server.py`; and how `web/mary/app.js` starts listening (grep for it).

**Andy:** wake words instead of buttons; "I don't want any buttons but the one button." Nothing goes to Google until the wake word is heard.

**Stage 1, spike and measure:**
1. **Try two on-device, offline, free options:**
   - **(a) openWakeWord:** check whether a usable "hey mary" / "hey sam" model can be made, for example with its synthetic-data training. If that needs hours of GPU, say so instead.
   - **(b) Vosk** with a small English model and a tiny grammar (`["hey mary", "hey sam", "[unk]"]`).
   - Never Porcupine, which needs a paid license.
2. **Build `wakeword.py`:** a background listener that shares the mic properly with `speech.py`. It pauses while Mary is talking or listening and resumes after. It works with the companion setting, so "Hey Sam" when Sam is chosen; "Hey Mary" can be an alias.
3. **Measure on this laptop** (scratch data only): CPU % while idle-listening, RAM, the model download size, and how long the wake takes. Test against your own recorded or synthesized samples, including sentences with "Mary" in them that shouldn't wake her ("I'm going to marry…").
4. **Add a "try it" command** for Andy, for example `python tools/wake_try.py`. It prints big "HEARD YOU" lines and counts false wakes over a few minutes, so he can test it across his room with his TV on.
5. **Stop:** commit with the subject `WAITING: M6 wake word spike ready for Andy to try`. The body has the numbers, which option you recommend, the exact try command, and what he should test.

**Stage 2 (after the hub messages you with Andy's results):** wire the chosen option into Mary.
- The server route, plus the page hook: a wake starts her listening (and later her Live session).
- A small "I'm listening" glow, with no button.
- An off switch in "Things Mary can do", or as a setting.
- Bundle the model in `packaging/build.py`.
- Tests with mocked audio.
- Then commit with the subject `DONE: M6 wake word`.

**Model:** Opus xhigh for the mic sharing and threading; Sonnet for the rest.
