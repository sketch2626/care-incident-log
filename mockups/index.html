My Care Notes: three front page ideas

Each one opens as a small working mockup. Pick a group or tap “Just tell me what happened”, then Save.

  • A. Big tilesA warm 3 by 3 grid of big square tiles, each with a large picture and a short count.
  • B. One big listOne column of full-width, extra-tall buttons: the simplest layout, best for very low vision and screen readers.
  • C. Tiles with a summaryA plain-language summary of the month sits on top of the tiles, for people who want the bigger picture.

All three use made-up sample data.
